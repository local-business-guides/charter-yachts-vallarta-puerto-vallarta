Private yacht planning with direct contact

Charter Yachts Vallarta runs private yacht charters in Bahía de Banderas, covering Puerto Vallarta, Marina Vallarta, Nuevo Vallarta, La Cruz, Punta Mita and Yelapa. We help guests choose a yacht and arrange outings for birthdays, proposals, anniversaries, family escapes, parties, small corporate groups and fishing adventures.

Every trip includes a professional crew and concierge-style planning. Options include jet skis, water toys, onboard massage and tailored food and beverage service. Discuss your preferences before booking so you understand the arrangements proposed for your group.

Talk through the details with Kyle

You can book directly with Kyle and ask questions about the yacht and your plans. Have your preferred date, guest count and occasion ready when you get in touch. It's also helpful to mention dietary needs, activity requests and any surprise you're organizing.

Confirm your trip's meeting point and boarding instructions during planning. The address below is the business contact address and shouldn't be treated as a confirmed departure point.

Puerto Vallarta Yacht Charter Service Areas

Private charters around Bahía de Banderas

Charter Yachts Vallarta plans private yacht outings in the Puerto Vallarta area and Bahía de Banderas. The locations below are part of that planning coverage. Mention the places that interest you when you inquire, then confirm the proposed route and departure details for your trip.

Puerto Vallarta

Puerto Vallarta is the business's listed location and a focus of its private yacht charter service. If you're staying here, share your plans and ask where your group should meet for the charter you've selected.

Marina Vallarta

Marina Vallarta is included in the areas served. Bring it up if it's relevant to your travel arrangements, and confirm the exact boarding instructions rather than relying on an area name alone.

Nuevo Vallarta

Charter planning also covers Nuevo Vallarta. Tell Kyle if your group is staying there or wants to discuss it as part of the outing, so the proposed arrangements can address that request.

La Cruz

La Cruz is another location included in the charter planning area. Ask how it could fit the day you're considering and review timing before committing to a particular plan.

Punta Mita

For requests involving Punta Mita, explain your preferred date, group size and reason for the outing. Those details help frame the discussion about the yacht and proposed schedule.

Yelapa

Yelapa is also included in the areas Charter Yachts Vallarta serves. If it's on your wish list, mention it early and ask how a visit could fit your charter arrangements.
